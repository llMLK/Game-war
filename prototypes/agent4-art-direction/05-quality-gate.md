# Visual quality gate

Demanding review rubric • All image scores and approvals currently **PENDING**

## Hard gates before scoring

Reject and repair before assigning a passing score if any of these is true:

- A visible period error changes the city's identity: Ottoman Constantinople, later fantasy/medieval keep, artillery bastion, late ship, or a confidently asserted invented historical feature.
- Principal landmark or geographic relationships contradict the approved guide.
- A market/port upgrade removes an existing wall, moves its gate, heals damage without state support, or fabricates a geographically impossible port.
- A compact asset has false transparency, visible matte fringe, incompatible camera/shadows or blocked road/shore connectors.
- Mobile readability can be demonstrated only by enlarging the image beyond the stated normal zoom.
- Required provenance/reference rights checks are absent for reused source imagery.

Hard technical failure cannot be offset by a gorgeous detail illustration. Equally, a technically clean sprite cannot pass without artistic distinction.

## Weighted rubric: 100 points

Rate each category 0–5. Weighted points = weight × rating / 5. A rating of 0 means absent/contradictory, 1 unusable, 2 major revision, 3 competent but below ambition, 4 meets this direction, 5 exceptional and consistently evidenced. Use the observable anchors below, not adjectives alone.

| Criterion | Weight | 2 — revision required | 4 — meets direction | 5 — exceptional evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Artistic distinctiveness | 14 | Generic strategy castle with gold frame | Five signature devices visible inside the painting | Unlabeled comparison identifies a coherent atlas family without copied motifs |
| Architectural richness | 9 | Many small repeated buildings; weak hierarchy | Landmark, districts and service/civic voids differentiated | Richness derives from structural relationships at both scales, with no filler clutter |
| Historical plausibility | 12 | Mixed eras or unsupported details presented as fact | H/R/I card complete, dated exclusions observed | Reviewer can trace significant choices and uncertainty remains honestly visible |
| Landmark recognition | 9 | Generic dome/tower needs label | Broad landmark structure recognizable without label at 64 px | Recognition survives 48 px, grayscale and crowded map placement |
| Composition / silhouette | 11 | Uniform roof carpet or central pile | Asymmetric focal mass, entry route, quieter secondary fields | Eye path, terrain and negative space remain coherent across crop/LOD changes |
| Material quality | 8 | All surfaces have same plastic sheen | Stone, tile, plaster, timber and cloth read differently in C | Material transitions reinforce weight and scale without adding map noise |
| Painterly craftsmanship | 9 | Filtered render, universal outline or uncontrolled texture | Deliberate edge hierarchy, broad pigments, selected fine marks | Every simplification improves form; no accidental muddy or over-sharpened areas |
| Controlled ornament | 5 | Gold everywhere or arbitrary late motifs | Gilding obeys area limits and hierarchy | Ornament enriches the focal path while architecture dominates |
| Mobile readability | 10 | Needs zoom/detail card to identify development | 4/5 human participants distinguish advanced state at normal phone zoom | Tier and facility changes remain legible in crowded/grayscale conditions |
| Reusable asset feasibility | 7 | Attractive indivisible illustration only | Registered slots, clean alpha, compatible masks and plausible budget | Three cumulative upgrades work cleanly with measured target-device results |
| Cross-image / tier consistency | 6 | Changed camera, landmark or lost upgrades | A–E share camera, light, palette and invariant landmarks | Overlays prove registration and tier expansion preserves architectural identity |
| **Total** | **100** | | | |

Proposed pass threshold: **at least 85/100**, no category below 3, and distinctiveness, historical plausibility, mobile readability and consistency each at least 4. Owner approval is independently mandatory and can reject a technically passing image. The score is a structured critique, not objective proof of beauty.

A alone cannot earn final scores for mobile readability, modular reuse or multi-image consistency: mark those **not yet evaluated**, report an incomplete review and never redistribute their weights to announce an 85+ pass. Formal package acceptance requires the relevant images, layers and tests.

## Repeatable review sequence

1. **Blind first impression:** show new artwork with no city name or style explanation, at normal size. Ask what feature dominates and what civilization/settlement role it suggests. Record verbatim responses before prompting.
2. **Silhouette and grayscale:** inspect solid silhouette and grayscale reductions at 48/64/96 CSS px, plus 32 px stress LOD. Screen-capture actual display size; retain DPR metadata. Confirm tier differences survive without gold or faction color.
3. **Architecture/history:** inspect the large image against the dated reference card. Trace roof support, gate passages, shoreline contact, landmark form and excluded periods. Note R details still needing specialist review.
4. **Composition/materials:** compare the normal view, enlarged crop and detail image. Ask whether additional marks explain material/space or merely increase density. Re-check quiet areas after texture is applied.
5. **Continuity:** overlay registered B/D/E guides; compare landmark contour, gate/shore sockets, fixed wall masks and light. Compare C's topology rather than demanding pixel equality at its expanded spatial scale.
6. **Gameplay context:** composite onto representative map terrain with neighbors, routes, armies and selection marks. Confirm roads enter gates and the foreground does not hide essential UI. RTL must leave artwork unmirrored.
7. **Technical evidence:** validate alpha against pale/dark/water backgrounds, export hashes, source alignment, valid state coverage, bytes and device timing. Inspect compression damage at display size and at edges.
8. **Owner review:** deliver the exact version, scorecard, defects and actual phone-scale comparisons. Record approval or requested changes. No response, model self-rating or passing automated check is approval.
