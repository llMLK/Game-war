# Historical and stylistic reference register

13 October 2026 • Initial campaign horizon: 715 CE • Research for art direction, not an archaeological reconstruction certificate.

## How to use the evidence

H means supported at the scope stated; R means plausible reconstruction; I means deliberate artistic treatment. An H building can contain R roofs and I scale emphasis. Keep these labels beside the actual design decision in the working file. Museum/archaeological sources establish context; a secondary specialist site helps locate plans and distinguish construction phases. Do not silently upgrade secondary evidence to excavation certainty.

All links below were searched or opened during this assignment. Source coverage and limitations are explicit. Images linked on a page were not automatically licensed or downloaded, and not every linked plan was visually inspected. No external reference image is redistributed in this package.

## Constantinople: the first research plate

Before painting, prepare a source-annotated plan with north, coastline and only six groups: western land defense; northern waterfront; southern waterfront; principal church; Hippodrome; imperial precinct. This prevents spectacular but contradictory compositions. A modern base map can establish broad relationships but cannot establish an ancient shoreline to the meter.

| Feature | Classification | Decision for first concepts |
|---|---|---|
| Landward layered defense | H at system level; R for a chosen segment in 715 | Give it depth and unequal tower rhythm; keep it on the landward edge |
| Sea walls and openings | H broadly; R for exact selected condition | Lower continuous coastal defense with believable access; no dry-land moat in the harbor |
| Hagia Sophia | H for existence and major architectural type | Keep its recognizable broad structure; reconstruct minor period details conservatively |
| Hippodrome / imperial district | H for major urban institutions; detailed shown massing R pending a dated plan | Use an elongated civic void and lower precinct; do not invent a towering castle |
| Domestic neighborhoods | R | Irregular roof groups, courts, mixed open/occupied areas; no exact population claim |
| Market/stores/barracks in specific positions | R | Put functions beside plausible routes, without naming a reconstructed court as an excavated fact |
| Harbor vessels | H for maritime activity, R for each complete depicted vessel | Few small low hulls; no towering stern castles, gunports or complicated later rigs |
| Roof color, awning colors, incidental clothing | R / I | Follow the common palette; omit fine claims without evidence |
| Landmark scale / compressed distances | I | B permits at most 1.35× linear landmark emphasis versus its approved schematic blockout; A/C at most 1.15×; preserve adjacency |
| Border, gold flecks, pigment harmony | I | Explicit atlas presentation, not an excavated building finish |

The scale limits compare against the approved **schematic art blockout**, not a historically measured full-city scale. If the city cannot read within them, simplify surrounding masses or recompose; do not progressively inflate the landmark.

## Period exclusions and uncertainty policy

Constantinople exclusions: Ottoman minarets and mosques; Topkapı-like palatial profiles; Yedikule additions; later palatial/façade forms copied from modern photographs; artillery bastions; late heraldry; Renaissance ships. Preserve ecclesiastical identity with researched shapes rather than a forest of large crosses. A small cross finial is a reconstructive choice needing dated comparison, not proof of an exact surviving roof fixture.

Umayyad exclusions: modern Damascus as a direct exterior model; ubiquitous blue-tile domes; late muqarnas; four-iwan compositions used as a generic early mosque; Ottoman minarets; later Andalusian palaces. A later example may inform craft only when labeled as such.

Frontier exclusions: Sarkel in 715, romantic wooden onion-tower skylines, universal yurts for every settled community, invented uniform religious insignia. No strong claim about Samandar/Balanjar's precise layout is justified by this initial research. Their production briefs need a dedicated archaeologist-reviewed dossier.

When sources disagree, record both interpretations and prefer the least specific image that preserves local character. Do not resolve a gap by generating several plausible-looking pictures and voting on historical truth.

## Reference-board instructions for the artist

Prepare separate sheets after direction approval:

1. **Architecture sheet:** one plan and one silhouette per chosen monument, annotated by date/phase. Remove later additions from the working drawing only with a traceable reason.
2. **Material sheet:** stone/brick junction, roof/eave, plaster loss, timber/cloth. Study how material behaves; avoid copying modern dirt and restoration scars indiscriminately.
3. **Manuscript craft sheet:** a small number of dated examples, annotated for edge, pigment and gold placement. No blanket prompt such as “Persian miniature” that pulls the work toward a much later building vocabulary.
4. **Maritime sheet:** date each vessel separately, distinguish preserved hull from reconstructed rigging, and keep the Golden Horn and Theodosian harbor geographically separate.
5. **Negative sheet:** authorized local V1/V2 screenshots plus annotated later-period pitfalls. This board diagnoses rejected choices, not a style reference to preserve.

Log institution, object/page title, stable link, date range, evidence scope, rights/reuse status and which design decision it affects. Keep reference assets and generated/painted production assets separately identified. External image reuse rights are **not cleared by this document**.

## Research still required before production approval

The art direction is usable now, but exact 715 exterior attachments of Hagia Sophia, selected gate and sea-wall phases, palace elevations, neighborhood density, and dated vessel rigs remain reconstruction tasks. Khazar city-specific evidence is particularly limited. The initial concept must carry these uncertainties in its caption/review sheet. A historical reviewer can require simplification before final asset approval; no historical uncertainty can be concealed by painterly atmosphere.
