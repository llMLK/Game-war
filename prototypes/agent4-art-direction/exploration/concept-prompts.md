# Three final exploration prompts

These are standalone prompts for the three creative directions. They supersede the conservative numeric art limits for this exploration only. Original production prompts A–E remain unchanged in `../03-concept-prompts.md`.

The A/C images used the corresponding text below. B used its original text, then one focused refinement edit (both exact executed prompts are preserved in `prompts/`). The standalone B text below consolidates that edit for future reuse; it is not presented as the literal single-call provenance of the refined image.

## A — Monumental Imperial Grandeur

Use case: historical-scene. Create one original landscape 3:2 exploration painting for Suyuf Al-Mamalik, not a game sprite or screenshot. Subject: Constantinople in 715 CE. Elevated view from southwest toward northeast: massive western land defenses in the lower-left foreground, Golden Horn along the northern upper-left side, Sea of Marmara along the southern right side, the peninsula tapering toward the upper-right. The city occupies a real peninsula, not an island. Hagia Sophia anchors the eastern core with its broad dome, semi-domes and weighty structural body; the elongated Hippodrome is southwest of it and the lower Great Palace precinct southeast toward Marmara. This shared topology must remain intelligible. Vary neighborhoods, terraces, courts and open gardens; modest working quays and a few low wooden boats. Boldly idealize monumentality, spatial rhythm and beauty while preserving architectural identity. No numerical cap on landmark emphasis or illustrative gold. No Ottoman minarets, Ottoman palaces, Gothic or fantasy spires, gunpowder bastions, galleons, later heraldry, modern objects, pseudo-writing, title, watermark or UI. No toy diorama, plastic 3D render or generic mobile-game castle. Do not depict the later siege.

Direction A — MONUMENTAL IMPERIAL GRANDEUR. Make an awe-inspiring architectural epic, a city whose accumulated weight feels imperial. A formidable succession of limestone towers and layered curtain walls sweeps from close foreground into the city; sunlight catches their worn crowns while gateways open into profound cool shadow. Give the wall great thickness, masonry alternating with restrained brick courses, and a believable grounded base. Behind it unfold deep, unequal urban layers, rich terracotta roofs, colonnaded courts and dignified civic terraces. Hagia Sophia rises as a commanding but structurally coherent culmination, with ample breathing room around its dome. Tiny figures at the gate reveal immense scale. Compress distances with pictorial intelligence; do not replace the city with one gigantic cathedral.

Paint with exceptionally assured architectural draftsmanship and sumptuous opaque tempera/oil-like planes: honeyed ivory, porphyry crimson, bronze, luminous terracotta and profound lapis shadows. Raking amber light from upper-left reveals carved thresholds, chipped marble, plaster repairs and tile ridges. Expanses of quiet stone make the intricate passages precious. Subtle manuscript gold catches selected pictorial accents within the painting; no ornamental frame is needed. The distinguishing experience is MASS, RHYTHM and MAJESTY: powerful large shapes with a wealth of purposeful detail, not haze hiding poor geometry. The image should reward a long close look while striking immediately from across a room. Full painted terrain, water and sky; no transparency.

## B — Exquisite Illuminated Atlas

Direction B — EXQUISITE ILLUMINATED ATLAS. Imagine an extraordinary imperial folio painted by a consummate manuscript workshop, where an inhabited city becomes a treasure of pigment and crafted surfaces. Keep architecture volumetric and spatially convincing, but orchestrate the districts as elegant interlocking passages of color, with exquisite small courtyards, tiled roofs, deeply carved portals, fine shaded arcades and miniature harbor life. Let the wall rhythm and winding approaches guide the gaze to Hagia Sophia. Give the city lavish detail without equal emphasis everywhere; open water and courts provide calm intervals.

Use luminous matte tempera, transparent pigment glazes, astonishingly fine sable-brush drawing, shell-gold strokes and burnished gold-leaf passages. Lapis blue, malachite green, cinnabar-like red, imperial plum and warm ivory should feel jewel-like and rich rather than fluorescent. Gold belongs to the illustrated page and selected accents, not a claim that every historical roof was gilded. Weave exquisite late-antique-inspired vine scrolls, pearl-like dots and restrained geometric rhythms into a narrow purposeful margin and selected pictorial passages. The ornamental structure must feel inseparable from the composition, not a stock frame pasted around a generic render. No text or pseudo-calligraphy. Show subtle irregularity of hand-applied leaf and painted pigment without a dirty sepia filter.

The distinguishing experience is PRECIOUS CRAFT: marvelous drawing at the scale of a roof, a carved capital or a painted wave, supported by a coherent monumental city. Clear upper-left daylight, pearly cool shadows, saturated colored depths. A modern artistic synthesis inspired by historical craft, not a claim to reproduce a surviving 715 atlas. Full painted page; no transparency.

Make this paint language visible throughout the city itself: velvety pigment planes, minute shell-gold hatching at selected sunlit details and delicate hand-brushed lapis wave rhythms. Preserve volume and architectural richness without photographic microtexture. Use botanical/pearl ornament rather than large heraldic bird medallions, and exclude tall slender minaret-like towers. The border supports the city's craftsmanship rather than supplying its whole identity.

## C — Epic Historical Painting

Direction C — EPIC HISTORICAL PAINTING. Create a magnificent painterly panorama in which Constantinople emerges through layers of luminous marine air. Keep the architecture convincingly drawn but make light, atmosphere and the sensation of a vast inhabited world the protagonists. Near masonry is tactile and richly articulated; successive neighborhoods recede in violet-blue half-tones; warm light breaks across a few roof ridges, the great church and the harbor. A spacious sky and calm dark-blue Marmara frame the warm city, with the Golden Horn still legible to its north. The city must remain recognizable, not dissolve into fog.

Use the depth of a superb oil painting: transparent warm glazes over cool underpainting, scumbled pearl-grey atmospheric veils, loaded but selective impasto on illuminated stone and sea glints, and expressive brushwork whose scale changes from foreground to distance. Rich ultramarine, mineral turquoise, burnt sienna, rose-madder-like crimson, golden ochre and luminous ivory. Show marble catching reflected water light, soft linen, weathered timber and heavy lead-grey church roofs. Golden late-afternoon light from upper-left after passing coastal cloud, with deep but colorful shadow. The sense of gold comes primarily from radiant light; manuscript refinement survives in carefully chosen edges and a few precious accents, with no decorative border.

Heighten architectural presence through atmospheric scale, a sweeping diagonal composition and daring warm-cool orchestration rather than fantasy height. Small inhabitants and working boats suggest ordinary life within extraordinary grandeur. The distinguishing experience is IMMERSIVE LIGHT and PAINTERLY DEPTH. Full painted landscape, not a photograph, photobash, blurred render or map asset; no transparency.
