# Exploration round 1 — three interpretations

The owner's latest review approves the original research, planning and production framework as a foundation. It does **not** approve the final visual identity. This exploration is expressly authorized; the earlier document-only stop condition is superseded for these three concepts only.

The original seven documents and A–E production prompts remain unchanged. In this exploration, their gold-area percentages, landmark ratios and compact-LOD simplifications are provisional guidance, not hard creative limits. Historical identity and essential geography remain constraints. These paintings are not approved sprites and do not authorize Phase J integration.

| Direction | Artistic emphasis | What distinguishes it |
|---|---|---|
| A — Monumental Imperial Grandeur | Architectural mass, deep urban rhythm, immense defensive presence | Firm draftsmanship and monumental value structure |
| B — Exquisite Illuminated Atlas | Jewel-like pigment, meticulous painted surfaces, orchestrated gold and ornament | Precious manuscript craftsmanship within the whole image |
| C — Epic Historical Painting | Marine atmosphere, luminous warm/cool depth, expressive painterly edges | Historical world experienced through light and air |

All show Constantinople in 715 CE from the southwest with land walls west, Golden Horn north, Marmara south and the monumental core east. Their neighborhoods and minor attachments are interpretive reconstructions; grandeur and surface treatment are intentional idealization.

Tool confirmed and used: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, exposed here as `image_gen__imagegen`. Its documented capability is genuine raster generation from text, image editing and transparent output. This round completed three generations and one refinement edit of B. The three final paintings are opaque PNG files at 1536 × 1024 pixels. The tool exposes no model/version, seed or exact pixel-size parameter in this session, so none is invented. Output files, exact prompt paths and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [the generation record](generation-record.json).

The executed prompts are saved verbatim in `prompts/`; the reusable B prompt consolidates the generation and refinement instructions. No downloaded artwork was supplied as an image-generation input; the source board informed human art direction. B's own initial generated image was the input to its refinement edit. Later-period technique references are never architectural evidence for 715.

## Recommendation after owner visual review

The tool has already produced the actual comparison images. Use these three final files for the next visual decision. Their differences in architecture, pigment and atmosphere should be judged alongside the specific historical limitations in the Arabic review.

After a direction is selected and further work is authorized, use that selected image as the style reference for a focused artist paintover or image edit. First resolve the palace façades, land/sea-wall hierarchy, minor towers and vessel details against dated evidence. Keep a common southwest viewpoint and the same landmark relationships, then lock the selected palette, edge handling, pigment texture and ornament vocabulary. A historically checked composition should precede any later production asset work; the existing A–E prompts remain the future production system. No such follow-on work is part of this delivery.

Owner visual selection remains pending. No automatic numerical pass is assigned to unreviewed concept art.
