# Sid Meier's Civilization VI — هوية المدينة وكلفة التوسع
**Coverage: COMPLETE (documentary); confidence medium.** Windows release2016-10-21 [CV1](https://store.steampowered.com/app/289070/Sid_Meiers_Civilization_VI/?curator_clanid=6856721). Base districts separated from Rise and Fall/Gathering Storm and New Frontier's April2021 update. Mobile/console builds are not assumed equivalent to PC. No CivVII results used as CivVI fixes.

## A — System model
**EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE CV-C1/2:** districts occupy map space outside the city centre, with terrain-dependent adjacency benefits. Player chooses what to build and where; available tiles, production and future alternatives constrain the choice. Visible tile yields show benefits, future district needs are less obvious to a newcomer. Placement changes a city's lasting specialization and what can fit later; cities then support campaign victory. Exact adjacency tables not copied. [CV2 indexed manual](https://downloads.2kgames.com/civilization/vi/manuals/eu/CIV_VI_25TH_ONLINE_MANUAL_ENG.pdf)

## B — Why it works
**ANALYST-INFERENCE:** investing in a port-facing trade city differs from improving a frontier fortress. Opportunity cost arises from geography, not simply purchasing the same level in every city. An improvement should remain recognizable as part of the player's investment history.

## F/G — Transfer
**PROPOSED R08 ADAPT? H/J.** Input: existing city upgrades, site role and route/frontier access. Player chooses a local improvement or a strategic specialization; transition adds capability without silently deleting earlier buildings. Feedback explains unlocked function, net upkeep and visual stage. Anti-exploit: respec cannot refund and duplicate investment; old node identifiers retain ownership/value during future H conversion. Cost medium-high for schema/map, small for a read-only comparison panel.
No hex district puzzle or industrial campus transplanted to715; site-specific function is a design abstraction. Mobile/RTL uses city role badges plus text; no precision tile placement required. **T14:** compare two cities with different access; best choice changes for a disclosed reason. Converted old save retains every represented investment or explicit mapped equivalent.
