# Battle Brothers — خسائر تستمر وانسحاب له طريق
**Coverage: COMPLETE (documentary); confidence medium-high on design history.** PC full release2017-03-24 [BB1](https://battlebrothersgame.com/release-date-march-24th-2017/). Scope:2016 injury design,2017 review,2018 retreat proposal,2025 PC1.5.1.4/5 notes. No console parity/current latest claim.

## A — System model
**EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE BB-C1/2:** mercenaries persist between battles; death removes a character, temporary/permanent injuries affect future service. Player chooses contract, equipment and battle exposure under wages, recovery and replacement costs. Feedback is an injured/dead roster plus loot/payment; opponent strength and rolls create risk. Health returning is not equivalent to lost capability returning. [BB2](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-79-progress-update-injury-mechanics/)

## B — Why it works
**ANALYST-INFERENCE:** the relevant outcome is company survival/net capability, not victory alone. Saving a veteran or declining an unattractive contract can be a meaningful success. Persistent cost gives retreat an economic and personal role.

## C/D — Failure and opposition
**EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE BB-C4:** Ian Birnbaum praised tactics while criticizing tutorial and inventory friction; his example of losses consuming contract profits illustrates a possible recovery loop, not a measured inevitable death spiral. **BB-C5:** a Steam repair discussion asks for less swapping and repair-all, but does not establish2026 feature absence. **BB-C3:** blog commenters welcome retreat changes while disagreeing about revealing recruit traits. [review](https://www.pcgamer.com/battle-brothers-review/) · [player feedback](https://steamcommunity.com/app/365360/discussions/0/3124866695246728923/)
**ANALYST-INFERENCE FM-ATTRITION-SPIRAL/FM-MICRO:** losses reduce earning capability, forcing risky jobs to pay recovery; equipment maintenance can overwhelm the original tactical choice.

## E — Response
**EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE BB-C3:**2018-09-21 developer design replaces a teleport-to-safety option and tedious manual edge movement with automatic physical retreat toward map edges. It still allows casualties and includes a difficulty-scaled defence modifier. This is announced behaviour, not our verified release replay; **UNKNOWN efficacy**, especially pathfinding under congestion. **BB-C6:**2025-03-20 adds rare-item sale confirmation; March24 fixes loot equip/load and other regressions. These address narrower friction, not proof inventory solved. [BB3](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-105-additions-changes/) · [BB6](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appgroupname=Battle+Brothers&appids=365360&enddate=1743416107&feed=steam_community_announcements)

## F/G — Transfer
**PROPOSED R09 ADAPT? G1A route proof; G2 persistence.** Input: actual surviving formations, route access, wounds, supplies and commander status. Player orders covered retreat or continues pursuing objective; transition moves survivors on legal paths and records casualties/captures from events. Recovery later consumes time/resources on safe ground. Feedback distinguishes lost objective from annihilation and shows personnel conservation. Risk: safest play becomes endless disengagement; account for time/objective/supply lost, not magical damage. Do not import per-mercenary armour repair or difficulty-only retreat buffs.
715/mobile: banner/commander aggregates, recovery panel with choices rather than dozens of inventory slots. Save-dependent cost medium-high. **T11/T13:** orderly withdrawal with many survivors preserves them; reload/apply again cannot heal, duplicate loot or reapply deaths. Compare blocked and open escape paths.
