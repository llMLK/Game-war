# Old World — الأوامر ذات الكلفة البديلة
**Coverage: COMPLETE (documentary); confidence medium-high.** PC full release 2021-07-01 [OW4](https://www.gamespress.com/zh-TW/Old-World-the-historical-strategy-title-from-Soren-Johnson-and-Mohawk-); Steam launch is a later edition date, not the original release. Scope: 2021 design/review contrasted with release update151 (2026-10-07); no playthrough.

## A — System model
**EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE OW-C1:** a shared Orders pool pays for movement and other commands. Unit fatigue constrains concentrating repeated moves; roads improve order efficiency. Player sees remaining orders, units and map; enemy intentions remain uncertain. Spending on one front leaves less for workers or another front. State changes consume orders and move/build/act; renewal creates a turn-scale allocation cycle. Victory and character succession act over a campaign. Exact production/fatigue formulas vary by rule version and are not imported. [OW1](https://www.designer-notes.com/old-world-designer-notes-1-orders/)

## B — Why it works
**ANALYST-INFERENCE:** an idle unit can be a good decision because its action competes with a better one. Empire growth creates more choices without requiring all pieces to act. Roads gain military and civilian value. This works only while the pool remains scarce enough to create alternatives and the player understands what was forgone.

## C/D — Failure and counterevidence
**EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE OW-C3/4:** Fraser Brown praised prioritization and reactive AI but criticized launch menus, information and repetitive unit movement, and found victory less memorable than the personal rivalry. This is one completed PC campaign, not an October2026 verdict. **OW-C2:** the designer records an unlimited-movement prototype that confused/concerned the team; fatigue was the compromise. That is first-hand design history, not a post-release bug. Counterargument: the same review found meaningful long-term relationships and undo valuable. [OW2](https://www.pcgamer.com/old-world-review/)
**ANALYST-INFERENCE:** FM-MICRO and FM-CONTROL-FRICTION can persist even after an action budget; an unexplained order cap can feel like artificial paralysis.

## E — Response and result
**EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE OW-C5:** update151 adds automatic pinning for notable characters, improves AI overseas expansion and fixes repeated ready events and undo issues. These target discoverability/repetition and AI, not proof that every launch complaint was solved. **UNKNOWN** overall UI/late-campaign fix; patch observed, no matched before/after play. [OW3](https://mohawkgames.com/2026/10/07/old-world-update-151/)

## F/G — Transfer and falsifiable idea
**PROPOSED R04, ADAPT?** retain orders until fulfilled/invalidated and show an exception inbox, rather than import a new universal command currency. Input: banner order, valid target, discovered obstacles. Player confirms intent and one fallback; transition continues it each round or suspends it with a cause. Feedback: active order, next action, blocked reason; cancellation/review remain available. Risk: stale orders walk into known danger; prevent silent target substitution and apply identical validity rules to AI.
715 fit: stylized formation intent, not evidence of an historical Orders currency. Mobile RTL: one selected formation panel, no hover. Dependencies G1A/B; changes to saved campaign orders wait G2. **Test T06:** compare repeated-command fixture against persistent intent; fewer repeat taps without reducing correct interventions. Falsify if players fail to notice blocked orders or cannot regain control.

## H/I — Evidence and judgment
OW1 primary design; OW2 independent first-hand critique/counterpraise; OW3 primary patch; OW4 release. Access2026-10-17; detailed metadata in sources.csv. No independent contemporary UI retest. **الخلاصة: تكييف استمرار النية، وتأجيل عملة أوامر عامة.** Strongest objection: exceptions may hide decisions players enjoy making; test comprehension before any adoption.
