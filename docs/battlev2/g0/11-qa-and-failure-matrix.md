## 4. Future 1,000+ battle and performance study

DEFERRED G2C, not executed. Candidate preregistered set: 6 missions × 5 geometry variants × 4 force/condition compositions × 10 seeds = **1,200 encounters**. Include open, hill/woods, river/bridge, pass, gate/breach environments where mission-compatible; filter invalid combinations explicitly and replace to retain ≥1000 valid cases. Each fixture stores initial troop/resource/commander state, mission rules, content/rules build and seeds. Within each cell test manual-order replay/headless parity and paired stronger-force/cohesion perturbations; these are additional comparisons, not mislabeled unique battles. Include cautious/aggressive/withdrawal policies, blocked routes, zero ammo, fatigue, both withdrawals and commander loss.

Collect outcome by mission, control, casualties/captives/supplies, termination reason, loops/stalls, command delays, failed-route frequency, event/hash mismatch and strategy dominance. Compare distribution/confidence intervals for paired perturbations; more men need not win every case because congestion can hurt. Investigate systematic counterintuitive results with replays; do not average away conservation/parity failures. Every invariant failure blocks its gate regardless of win rate. Preserve all failure seeds and commands, not only successful examples.

For named midrange phones and browser versions measure R baseline and implemented BattleV2: light and crowded campaign turns, encounters per turn, median/p95 duration, frame-stall distribution, peak memory and replay bytes; include Arabic RTL and background/resume. Workload counts, repetitions and thermal conditions recorded before interpreting. No production latency budget is approved in G0. Culling/reduced animation/batching may help without changing physics. See10 future-performance. Human fun gate remains distinct from this study.
