Prioritize five decisions for G0 review: (1) physical control plus no viable contest as pilot objective (P01/P02); (2) continuous fixed-point map and five banners as prototype direction (P03/P06); (3) the proposed formation interactions and transparent order delays as the initial tuning approach (P04/P11); (4) short execution/review cadence with persistent commands (P05); (5) the RTL phone flow (P07). Answer approve/change/needs demonstration with optional comments. G0 approval should state which proposals are accepted for experimentation; **it is not proof of fun or permission to deploy**. Remaining P08–P10/P12/P14 decisions stay explicitly deferred to their gates, not tacitly approved by accepting G0. P13/P15 remain technical proposals to verify.

No work advances merely because a question is unanswered. This package ends at review. A later G1A assignment must explicitly authorize implementation; its owner phone test opens G1B, then a separate approved integration scope opens G2. Human questions are preserved in02/08/10.
